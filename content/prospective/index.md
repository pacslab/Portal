        We welcome new members at all academic stages, from BSc students to
        postdoctoral researchers. Our research focuses on advancing computing
        systems by enhancing performance, availability, reliability,
        cost-efficiency, power consumption, and runtime operations. Areas of
        interest include **Serverless Computing, Machine Learning Systems,
        Microservice Platforms, Cyber-Physical Systems, Cloud Software Systems,
        Operating Systems, and Distributed Ledgers**. Our approach combines
        mathematical modelling with large-scale cloud experimentation, and we
        frequently develop proofs-of-concept and prototypes. Industrial
        experience is a valuable plus, though not required.

        ## How to apply

        You may initiate the process by sending an email with the following specs:

        - **To:** pacs@yorku.ca
        - **Subject format:** `prospective-[BSc|MSc|PhD|Postdoc]-[Fall|Winter|Summer]-[Year]` (e.g., `prospective-PhD-Fall-2025`)
        - **Attachments:** CV and Transcripts

        Your CV may include the following:

        - Education
        - CGPA and the last two semesters' GPA
        - Interests and expertise
        - Language and GRE band score (if available)
        - Publications (if applicable)

        **Email content:** Briefly introduce yourself and state why you are
        interested in joining the PACS lab. Please note that you need to convince
        the director that you are familiar with and interested in the type of
        research that we do in the lab.

        ## What is next?

        By following the instructions, especially regarding the subject format,
        you'll ensure a prompt and timely response.

        ## Local Undergraduate Students

        We welcome BSc students at York University through the following
        research-oriented courses:

        - **EECS 4070 3.0** — Directed Studies
        - **EECS 4080 3.0** — Computer Science Project
        - **EECS 4088 6.0** — Computer Science Capstone Project
        - **EECS 4090 6.0** — Software Development Capstone Project

        We also accept undergraduate students to work on active projects during
        the summer through the following programs. The director will top up the
        following awards for admitted students to make a decent salary:

        - **NSERC** Undergraduate Student Research Award (USRA)
        - **Lassonde** Undergraduate Student Research Award (LURA)
